# SAMAI v11 trust and conversion audit

## Goal

Reduce uncertainty between understanding the service and sending a request. The pass must not invent clients, testimonials, metrics or delivery guarantees.

## Changes

- Added a compact mid-page decision block that explains what follows a request.
- Named the three subjects of the first conversation: context, boundaries and the next step.
- Stated that a ready-made strategy is not promised during the first conversation.
- Added a contextual call to action after the scope and responsibility evidence.
- Clarified that the form needs a name and a preferred contact method.
- Increased the readability of secondary trust, form and disclaimer text.

## Evidence standard

Trust is built with visible scope, roles, decision criteria and process. Client names, results and testimonials remain absent until verified source material is available.

## Release gate

The v11 browser gate checks the new content, CTA prefill, keyboard focus, readable body copy and horizontal overflow at 320, 390, 430 and 1440 pixels.
